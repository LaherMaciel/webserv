About the journal

Attention changes a walk.

Canopy Journal is a compact record of local paths, changing seasons, and one very unhurried trail companion.

The practice

Look slowly. Write plainly.

The journal began as a way to notice what changed between repeated walks. Each field note keeps the details simple: weather, movement, colour, and whatever made us pause.

The plain-text records are kept in the public field-file directory, alongside the stylesheet used by this site.
