Dispatch 08 / Late autumn

The path after rain.

A short record from the lower woodland loop, where familiar ground looked briefly rearranged.

The rain stopped before we reached the first gate, but every branch still carried it. Drops fell without wind, landing on the dry path one at a time. The woods sounded busy even though nothing seemed to move.

Scout chose the longer fork. Ferns leaned across the narrow section and darkened his coat as he passed. At the clearing, a robin moved between the same two fence posts for several minutes, keeping just far enough ahead to look as though it knew our route.

By the time we turned home, the low sun had found the wet leaves. The path was unchanged, but the light made it feel newly drawn. That is enough reason to walk it again tomorrow.
